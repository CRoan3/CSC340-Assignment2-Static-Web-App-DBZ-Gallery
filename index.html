Meet the Heroes of Dragon Ball

Goku

Species: Saiyan

Role: Martial Artist / Hero

Affiliation: Z Fighters

Goku is a Saiyan warrior raised on Earth. He trains relentlessly to become stronger and protect his family, friends, and planet Earth.

Details

Vegeta

Species: Saiyan

Role: Warrior / Rival

Affiliation: Z Fighters

Vegeta is the prince of the Saiyan race. He is one of Goku's greatest rivals, as well as one of his most powerful allies.

Details

Krillin

Species: Human

Role: Martial Artist

Affiliation: Z Fighters

Krillin is one of Goku's oldest friends and a skilled martial artist in the Z Fighters.

Details

Bulma

Species: Human

Role: Scientist / Inventor

Affiliation: Capsule Corporation

Bulma is a scientist and inventor whose technology has helped the Dragon Ball protagonists on many occasions. She is also the wife of Vegeta and the mother of Trunks and Bulla.

Details

Chi-Chi

Species: Human

Role: Martial Artist / Mother

Affiliation: Son Family

Chi-Chi is Goku's wife, Gohan and Goten's mother, and a former martial artist. She is known for her strong personality and dedication to her family.
